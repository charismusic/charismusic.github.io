Charis Music Apps Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 20 July 2026

Charis Music ("Charis Music", "we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting it.

This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile applications ("Apps") collect, use, and protect information when you use them.

This Privacy Policy applies to all mobile applications published by Charis Music, including current and future Android and iOS applications, unless an individual application provides its own Privacy Policy.

Information We Collect

Our Apps are designed to minimise the collection of personal information.

We do not require users to create an account or provide personal information in order to use our Apps.

However, some information may be collected automatically by Google services used within our Apps. Depending on the App and your device settings, this information may include:

  • Device information
  • IP address
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Diagnostic information
  • App performance information
  • Information related to the delivery and performance of advertisements

Charis Music does not use this information to identify individual users.

Advertising

Some Charis Music Apps display advertisements using Google AdMob.

Google AdMob may automatically collect and process certain information in order to display relevant advertisements, measure advertising performance, and prevent fraud.

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites and apps

Third-Party Services

  • Google Play Services
  • Google AdMob

These third-party services operate under their own privacy policies.

Children's Privacy

Many Charis Music Apps are designed for music education and may be used by children under the supervision of parents, teachers, or other responsible adults.

We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information associated with our Apps. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the Internet is completely secure.

Your Privacy Rights

If you are located in the United Kingdom, the European Economic Area (EEA), or another jurisdiction with applicable privacy laws, you may have certain rights regarding your personal information.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page.
